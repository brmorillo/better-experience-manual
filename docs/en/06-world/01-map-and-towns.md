# Map and towns

The game takes place in Knox County, Kentucky. Known towns: Muldraugh, West Point, Riverside, Rosewood, March Ridge and the big city of Louisville. Build 42 added new areas such as Brandenburg, Irvington, Ekron and Echo Creek.

Open the map with the **M** key. Maps found in the world reveal regions.

# Getting started

Welcome to **Better Experience - ALL In One**! This mod bundles several quality-of-life improvements for Project Zomboid Build 42.

## Where everything is

- **Right-click the floor or a piece of furniture** > **Better Experience - ALL In One**: organize your base, food by expiration, map search, GPS and this manual.
- **On the map** (M key): the **Assistant** button, with the **Items** and **Favorites / GPS** tabs.
- **Options** > **Mods** > **Better Experience - ALL In One**: every setting and the hotkeys.

## Hotkeys

No key is bound by default, so the mod never clashes with other mods. Pick yours in **Options > Mods > Better Experience - ALL In One > Hotkeys**. You can bind organizing, the food list, map search, favorites, the compass bar and this manual.

## What is in the mod

- **Base organizer**: tag your furniture with a category and your character puts everything away.
- **Food by expiration**: a list of what spoils first.
- **Item search on the map**: where was that knife you saw yesterday?
- **GPS and compass**: map favorites, navigation and a Skyrim-style compass bar.
- **Drink from bags**: the game's "Auto drink" also uses water in your bags.

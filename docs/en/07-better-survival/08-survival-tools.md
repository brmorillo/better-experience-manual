# Tools

## Gas Chainsaw

- In the crafting menu, on a surface: screwdriver, wrench, engine parts, 1 metal sheet, scrap metal and 4 screws.
- Cuts trees about 4 times faster than an axe and tires you much less.
- Comes without gas. Right click it > **Refuel Chainsaw** (uses 0.5 L from a gas can). A full tank is about 50 hits.
- VERY loud and sometimes jams (the action stops; just order it to chop again).

## Wheelbarrow

- In the crafting menu, on a surface: hammer, 4 planks, 8 nails, 1 metal sheet or bucket and 1 tire.
- Carry it with both hands. Holds a lot and the load weighs much less (logs, dirt, stones). You walk slower.

## Chopping Trees Is Tiring

- Every axe hit on a tree costs endurance and makes you a little thirsty.

# Traps

## Nail Board

- In the crafting menu, on a surface: hammer, 1 plank and 6 nails.
- Drop it on the ground. A zombie that steps on it falls, hurts its feet and crawls from then on.
- Breaks after 4 zombies.

## Tin Can Alarm

- In the crafting menu: 3 empty tin cans and 1 twine, thread, wire or rope.
- Drop it on the ground. When a zombie walks over it the cans rattle and you get a warning if you are nearby.

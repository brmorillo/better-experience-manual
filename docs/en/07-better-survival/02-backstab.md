# Stealth

Attacking a zombie from behind pays off.

- **Hit from behind**: every melee hit from behind is **critical** and deals **double** damage.
- **Surprise attack**: **crouched**, from behind and **before the zombie notices you**, the hit deals **4 times** the damage. It almost always kills.
- **Shove from behind**: always **knocks the zombie down**.

A message shows above your character when it counts. Firearms do not count.

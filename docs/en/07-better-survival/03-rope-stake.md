# Rope Stake

Holds a zombie in place. It stays alive, turns to whoever comes near and bites, but it can't leave the tile. A row of them makes a living fence of zombies.

## How to make it

- In the crafting menu: **1 stake** and **1 rope** (or sheet rope). No surface needed.
- With the stake in your inventory, right-click the ground > **Drive Rope Stake Into Ground**. The ground must be free, with no trench.

## How it works

- The first zombie that steps on the stake gets tied to it. Each stake holds one zombie.
- It can only move a little around the stake. Walk right next to it and you get bitten; 2 tiles away is safe.
- Other zombies walk past the fence normally. Put trenches in front to hold the rest.

## How to free it

- Kill the zombie.
- Or click the stake > **Pull Out Rope Stake**: the zombie is free and you get the stake back.

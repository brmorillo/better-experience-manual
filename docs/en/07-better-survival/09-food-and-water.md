# Food and Water

## Homemade Water Filter

- In the crafting menu: 1 bottle, 1 charcoal, 1 rag and a bag of dirt, sand or gravel (the bag is kept).
- With the filter in your inventory, right click a container with tainted water > **Filter Water**.
- Each filter cleans about 10 liters.

## Sun-Dry Meat

- In the crafting menu, on a surface: 1 piece of raw meat, 1 twine or rope and 2 branches or planks (kept).
- Makes jerky, which lasts a long time. No salt needed.

## Fish Trap

- In the crafting menu: 10 twigs and 1 twine, thread, rope or wire.
- Drop it on the ground right next to water. Every 8 hours it may catch a fish (up to 3).
- Right click the trap > **Check Fish Trap** to collect.

## Beehive

- In the crafting menu, on a surface: hammer, 4 planks, 6 nails and 1 jar of honey (lures the swarm).
- Drop it on the ground outdoors. Every 3 days there is a new jar of honey (up to 4).
- Right click it > **Collect Honey**.

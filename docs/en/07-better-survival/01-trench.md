# Trench

A ditch dug in the ground that knocks zombies down. With stakes inside, it also wounds and can kill.

## Digging

- You need a **shovel** (or a **hand shovel**, much slower) and **dirt or grass** ground, outside buildings and on the ground floor.
- Right-click the ground > **Shovel > Dig Trench**.
- The trench takes **3 stages**. Each stage takes about **1 in-game hour** with a shovel (2.5 h with a hand shovel).
- Each stage is hard work: it drains endurance and adds **fatigue**, **hunger** and **thirst**.
- You can stop between stages and come back later: the option becomes **Keep Digging Trench (stage 2/3)**. The hole shows on the ground at each stage.
- **Fill In Trench With Dirt (undo)** closes the ditch and gives the stakes back.

## Stakes

With a **stake** in your inventory, right-click the trench > **Place Stake**. Up to **3** fit per square, and they show inside the ditch.

## Cover (invisible trap)

- Make a **Trench Cover** in the crafting menu: **1 dirt bag** (fill it with a shovel), **3 grass tufts**, **6 twigs** (from trees) and **2 wood splinters**.
- Right-click the trench > **Hide Trench With Cover**. It looks almost like the grass around it: just a slightly darker tone you can spot if you look closely.
- Whoever steps on it (zombie or you) **breaks the cover** and falls. Cover it again to reuse it.
- **Remove Trench Cover** gives the cover back intact.

## Zombies

Every zombie that walks into the ditch falls. After falling, it can't fall again for **10 seconds**, so it can get across. A zombie that is already **crawling** passes over without falling or getting hurt.

- **No stakes**: it only falls.
- **1 stake**: 20% to become a crawler; 30% chance to lose 30% of its health.
- **2 stakes**: 55% to become a crawler; 50% chance to lose 70% of its health.
- **3 stakes**: always becomes a crawler and loses 95% of its health; 5% chance to die at once.

A zombie left with almost no health after the damage dies.

## You

Stepping into the trench knocks your character down too. It never kills outright, but it can hurt:

- **No stakes**: 30% chance of a cut or scratch on the chest, an arm or a hand.
- **1 stake**: 40% deep wound on a foot; 45% wound on the chest, an arm or a hand.
- **2 stakes**: 70% deep wound on a foot; 60% wound on the chest, an arm or a hand.
- **3 stakes**: always a deep wound on a foot; 75% wound on the chest, an arm or a hand.

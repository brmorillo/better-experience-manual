# Fire and Light

## Torch

- **Make Torch (fuel)**: 1 branch or long stick, 1 rag or cloth strip and 0.25 L of gasoline, alcohol, animal fat or strong liquor.
- **Make Torch (fat)**: the same, with lard, vegetable oil, olive oil or butter instead of the liquid.
- **Light Torch**: needs something that starts a fire (lighter, matches, flint and steel). The lit torch goes straight to your hand.
- A lit torch lights up the area and burns slowly. Double click it to put it out and keep the rest.

## Flint and Steel

- In the crafting menu: 1 flint (or sharpened stone), 1 piece of steel or iron (or a file) and 1 leather strip, cloth or twine.
- Lights campfires and torches like a lighter and lasts a long time. Fits in a wallet.

## Signal Smoke

- On a lit campfire, right click > **Make Signal Smoke**. Uses 3 grass tufts.
- For 1 hour the smoke calls zombies from very far away (150 tiles) to the campfire. Use it to pull a horde away from your base.

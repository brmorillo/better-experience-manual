# Salting and Smoker

Makes meat and fish last much longer.

## Salting

- In the crafting menu, on a surface (table, counter): **1 meat or 1 fish fillet** and **1 salt**.
- Works with steak, beef, pork chop, mutton chop, venison, chicken and rabbit.
- You get **Salted Meat** or **Salted Fish**. It gives a little Cooking experience.
- It still needs cooking before you eat it.
- It lasts much longer than fresh meat: meat stays fresh for 20 days, fish for 15.

## Building the smoker

- In the build menu, Cooking category: **6 long sticks or saplings**, **6 twine, ripped sheets, denim strips, leather strips or rope** and **2 logs**.
- It gives Cooking experience.

## Smoking

- Open the smoker and put in up to **20** **salted** meats or fish at once. It only takes salted food.
- Start smoking. It takes about 1 in-game day.
- You get **Smoked Meat** or **Smoked Fish**, ready to eat without cooking.
- It lasts a long time: meat stays fresh for 60 days, fish for 45.

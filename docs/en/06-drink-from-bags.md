# Drink from bags

The game's **Auto drink** only uses water from your main inventory. With the mod, your character also drinks water carried in a backpack, a fanny pack or any bag.

## How to use

1. Turn on **Auto drink** in the game options (or right-click a bottle > **Enable auto drink**).
2. Carry water in any bag.

When thirst kicks in, your character takes a sip of the same size the game uses.

## What it drinks

- Only **water** and **carbonated water**.
- Nothing **sealed** and nothing you **already know is tainted**.
- If there is water in your main inventory, the game drinks from it first.

## Option

**Options > Mods > Better Experience - ALL In One > Survival > Auto-drink from bags**.

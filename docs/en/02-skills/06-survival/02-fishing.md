# Fishing

**Category:** Survival. **In the Portuguese game:** Pesca.

## What the game says

> Increases your chance of catching a fish, the quality of your catches, and makes your fishing equipment less likely to break.

## How to train

Fish. Read the Fishing book for your level first: it multiplies the experience you get.

## What you need

- A **Fishing Rod** or a **Makeshift Fishing Rod**, with line.
- A **hook**. Without a hook, no fish bites. From worst to best: **Paperclip** (bites 20% rarer), **Nails** (normal), **Fishing Hook**, **Forged Fishing Hook** or **Bone Fishing Hook** (bites 20% more often).
- **Bait**. Each fish likes different bait (see below).

## Where to cast

- **Fish schools**: water that **bubbles** has fish. The fishing window shows the **Fishing Abundance** of the spot. Schools move every day.
- **Water with no fish**: bites are very rare and almost always bring **trash**. When a fish comes, it is small.
- **Near the shore**: if the bobber lands within **7 tiles** of the shore, the chance of medium and big fish is cut in half and bites take **twice** as long. Cast far: big lakes and wide rivers are the best places.
- **Near you**: if the bobber is less than **3 tiles** from your character, bites take **three times** as long.
- You can cast up to about **15 tiles** away.
- At low levels the bobber lands a bit off from where you aimed (up to 1.5 tiles at level 0). At level 10 it lands almost exactly there.
- In Build 42 every fish lives in both rivers and lakes.

## What makes a fish bite

Each bite attempt depends on all of this together:

- **Fish in the spot**: more than 25 fish is best (1.5x). 10 to 25 is normal. Fewer than 10 halves it. No fish, almost nothing (0.1x).
- **Temperature**: 15 to 30 °C is best. 0 to 15 °C or 30 to 40 °C drops it by a quarter. Below 0 °C or above 40 °C halves it. Below -10 °C only a quarter is left.
- **Weather**: **rain** helps (+20%). Heavy **fog** or strong **wind** hurt (-20%).
- **Time**: from **4 to 6 AM** and **6 to 8 PM** bites are 20% more frequent.
- **Hook**: see above.

> When a fish bites, the game goes back to normal speed by itself.

## What changes at each level

Your level changes the **size** of the fish, **which species** you can catch and how much **trash** you get.

**Chance of small / medium / big fish** (away from the shore):

- Level 0: 95% / 5% / 0%
- Level 1: 85% / 15% / 0%
- Level 2: 75% / 24% / 1%
- Level 3: 70% / 25% / 5%
- Level 4: 60% / 30% / 10%
- Level 5: 48% / 40% / 12%
- Level 6: 35% / 45% / 20%
- Level 7: 25% / 45% / 30%
- Level 8: 20% / 40% / 40%
- Level 9: 15% / 40% / 45%
- Level 10: 10% / 40% / 50%

Near the shore, half of the medium and big chance turns into small. Example: level 8 becomes 60% / 20% / 20%.

**Species from each level on** (by the fish's maximum weight):

- Level 0: **Bluegill**, **White Crappie**, **Black Crappie**, **Redear Sunfish**, **Green Sunfish**, **Yellow Perch** and **Little Bait Fish**.
- Level 1: **White Bass**.
- Level 2: **Spotted Bass** and **Sauger**.
- Level 4: **Smallmouth Bass** and **Channel Catfish**.
- Level 5: **Largemouth Bass**.
- Level 6: **Freshwater Drum**.
- Level 7: **Striped Bass** and **Walleye**.
- Level 8: **Flathead Catfish**, **Muskellunge** and **Paddlefish**.
- Level 9: **Blue Catfish**.
- Level 10: **Alligator Gar**.

**Less trash**: from level 5 you get 20% less trash, from level 7 40% less and from level 9 80% less.

**Stronger line**: each level raises how much pull the line takes before it snaps.

## Bait

Each species has favorite baits. If no fish you can catch likes the bait, you only get trash.

- **Small fish** (Bluegill, Crappies, Sunfish, Yellow Perch): insects (**Cricket**, **Grasshopper**), **Tadpole**, **Snail**, **Worm** and leeches.
- **Basses and Sauger**: **Worm**, **Maggots**, **Tadpole**, **Crayfish** and leeches.
- **Walleye and Striped Bass**: **Leech** is the best.
- **Freshwater Drum**: **Dough** and plant baits (bread, cheese, corn).
- **Catfish**: **Canned Dog Food (Open)** is the best, then meat.
- **Muskellunge and Alligator Gar**: **Little Bait Fish**.
- **Paddlefish**: almost never bites, whatever the bait.
- **Artificial lures** (**Jig Lure**, **Minnow Lure**): work for many fish, with an average chance.

**How long bait lasts**: at level 0, natural bait is lost on 95% of the fish you catch. At level 10, on 45%. Artificial lures are almost never lost. Catching trash does not use up bait.

## Predator fish

**Blue Catfish**, **Flathead Catfish**, **Muskellunge** and **Alligator Gar** only bite if the **line is taut** at the moment of the bite. Keep the line tight while you wait.

## Chum

**Chum** thrown in the water attracts fish to the spot. The base alone attracts nothing: add bait to it to make it stronger.

## Occupations and traits that help

- **Fishing Guide** (occupation): starts with Fishing +3 and already knows how to make and fix rods, make fishing nets and chum.
- **Angler** (trait): Fishing +1. Knows how to make and fix rods and make chum.
- **Former Scout** (trait): Fishing +1, along with other skills, and the same recipes as Angler.

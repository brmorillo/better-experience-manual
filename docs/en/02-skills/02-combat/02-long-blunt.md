# Long Blunt

**Category:** Combat. **In the Portuguese game:** Contundentes Longos.

## What the game says

> Increases attack speed and damage when using long blunt weapons, and reduces their muscle strain.

## How to train

Hit zombies with long blunt weapons (a baseball bat, for example).

# Long Blade

**Category:** Combat. **In the Portuguese game:** Lâminas Longas.

## What the game says

> Increases attack speed and damage when using long blade weapons, and reduces their muscle strain.

## How to train

Hit zombies with long blade weapons (machete, katana).

# Spear

**Category:** Combat. **In the Portuguese game:** Lanças.

## What the game says

> Increases attack speed and damage when using spear-type weapons, and reduces their muscle strain.

## How to train

Hit zombies with spears.

## What changes at each level

- **Level 7**: spears reach **20% farther**. The circle from **Highlight combat area** already shows the new reach.

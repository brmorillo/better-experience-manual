# Short Blunt

**Category:** Combat. **In the Portuguese game:** Contundentes Curtos.

## What the game says

> Increases attack speed and damage when using short blunt weapons, and reduces their muscle strain.

## How to train

Hit zombies with short blunt weapons (a hammer, for example).

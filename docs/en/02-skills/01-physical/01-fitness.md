# Fitness

**Category:** Physical. **In the Portuguese game:** Aptidão Física.

## What the game says

> Increases melee attack speed, and speed and success chance of climbing. Decreases exhaustion rate.

## How to train

Do the exercises from the exercise menu and stay active: running and fighting also train it.

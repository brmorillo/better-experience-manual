# Food by expiration

A window with all the food you carry and keep at your base, from what spoils first to last.

## How to open

Right-click > **Better Experience - ALL In One** > **Food by expiration**, or use the hotkey.

## What each column shows

- **Food**: the item name.
- **Where**: **On you** or the furniture at your base.
- **Prep**: **Raw**, **Ready** or **Cook it!** (raw meat and fish, for example).
- **Spoils in**: days left. **(stale)** means it is no longer fresh. **Non-perishable** for canned and dry food.

> Tip: open the list before cooking and use what is at the top first.

# FAQ

## My character does not store anything

Check that your furniture is tagged with a category and inside the **base radius** (option). Favorite, equipped and hotbar items are never moved.

## Map search cannot find an item I know is there

The search only knows containers you have **seen up close**. Walk by the furniture and open the loot panel once.

## The compass bar does not show

Hold the compass in your hand, or turn off **Compass must be held** in the options. Also check that **Compass bar at the top of the screen** is on and that you did not hide the bar with the hotkey.

## I cannot create favorites

Favorites need a **powered GPS** in your inventory. The compass only shows favorites that already exist.

## Does it work in multiplayer?

The mod is made for **single player**. Some parts may work on servers, but they are not tested.

## Can I add the mod to an existing save?

Yes. Categories and favorites are stored in your save.

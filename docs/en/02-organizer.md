# Base organizer

The assistant puts items away in the right furniture of your base. You only say what goes where.

## 1. Tag your furniture

Right-click a piece of furniture (fridge, cabinet, crate, shelf...) > **Better Experience - ALL In One** > **Category**. Choose:

- **A whole group**, like **All Fridge** or **All Tools**: takes anything from that group.
- **A subcategory**, like **Canned and preserved** or **Ammo**: takes only that.
- **Inbox**: a place to drop things without thinking. The assistant empties it later.
- **Trash / spoiled**: rotten food and junk go there.
- **General**: anything without a better place.

Right-click an item in your inventory: the menu shows **Assistant stores in: ...**.

## 2. Start organizing

Right-click anywhere > **Better Experience - ALL In One**:

- **Store my inventory at base**: your character walks to each piece of furniture and stores what you carry.
- **Organize the whole base**: also empties the inboxes and fixes items stored in the wrong place. It makes several trips, respecting how much weight you can carry.
- **Stop organizing**: stops at any time (or press the hotkey again).

## Where each item goes

An item first looks for furniture with its exact subcategory, then its group and, last, **General**. Examples:

- Raw meat: **Raw meat** > **All Freezer** > fridge > pantry > **General**.
- Hammer: **General tools** > **All Tools** > materials > **General**.

If there is no place at all, the item stays with you.

## What is never moved

**Favorite**, **equipped** and **worn** items, items on the **hotbar** and **keys** always stay with you.

## Options

- **Base radius**: how many tiles from your character tagged furniture counts.
- **Floors above/below**: how many floors count as base (basements included).
- **Organize bag contents**: also stores what is inside your backpack.
- **Stop if zombies are near**: stops when a zombie shows up.

# GPS and compass

Mark places on the map, navigate to them and see your points on a compass bar at the top of the screen, Skyrim style.

## Compass

Hold the game's **compass** in your hand (the off hand works). A bar shows up at the top of the screen with the cardinal points and your **favorites**. When you look towards a favorite, its name shows. **^** and **v** mean the point is on another floor.

The compass **does not create favorites** and does not show distance. For that you need the GPS.

## GPS

The **portable GPS** is a new item that uses a regular battery. Where to find it:

- Electronics and camping stores.
- Military storage and survival kits.
- The glove box of about **1%** of cars.

To swap the battery, right-click the GPS, like a flashlight.

## What the GPS does

- **Save a favorite**: right-click the map > **GPS: save this point**, or right-click the floor > **GPS: save my position**.
- **Navigate**: right-click the map > **GPS: navigate here**. A green line shows on the map and an arrow on screen shows the distance.
- **Record your track**: on the map, **Favorites / GPS** tab > **Record track**. Your path is drawn in blue.
- **Manage favorites**: the **Favorites / GPS** tab lets you rename, delete and navigate.

Navigation is a **straight line**: the GPS shows the direction, not the route along the roads.

## Battery

The battery only drains while you **navigate** or **record** a track. With the default setting, a battery lasts 48 in-game hours of continuous use.

## Options

- **Require a powered GPS**: when off, everything works without the item.
- **Battery life**, **Cars with a GPS in the glove box (%)**.
- **Compass bar at the top of the screen**, **Compass must be held**, **Favorites range on the bar**.

# Versions

What came in each mod version.

## 0.4.0

- **Mod features**: each part (organizer, food, map search, GPS, compass, drinking from bags, manual) can be turned off in the options. When off, it does not show up, does not run and writes nothing to your save.
- **GPS** (new item): favorites, destination with a map line and an on-screen arrow, track recording. The battery only drains while navigating or recording.
- **Compass bar** at the top of the screen, with the cardinal points and your favorites, while holding a compass or with the GPS on.
- A GPS in the glove box of a few cars (1% by default).
- Map item search in Portuguese and English.
- **Drink from bags**: the game's auto-drink also uses water in your bags.
- In-game **mod manual**, written by the community, with player tips and diagrams.

## 0.3.0

- **Map item search**: the mod records what was inside furniture you have seen and shows on the map where each item is.

## 0.2.0

- Two-level categories (group and subcategory). Food split into freezer, fridge and pantry.
- Rotten or burnt items go to the trash; raw food that was cooked goes to ready meals.

## 0.1.0

- **Base organizer**: tag furniture with a category and store your inventory or organize the whole base.
- **Food by expiration**: a list of what expires first.
- Options and hotkeys.

# Versions

What came in each mod version.

## 0.8.1

- The **Admin panel** is still off by default and only shows up with the option on. Now, the first time you open the panel in a save, the game asks first. If you confirm, the save is marked: from then on the panel is always available in that save, even if you turn the option off. Other saves do not change.

## 0.8.0

- **All nearby**: new button in the loot window's container column that shows in a single list the items of every nearby container and the floor. Taking an item takes it from its own container; **Loot All** goes through each box.
- **Inventory search**: **Search...** box in the title bar of the inventory and the loot window. No accents needed, name in English or the game's language, category and several words.
- **Freshness bar on food**: the food tooltip shows a freshness bar and how long until it goes stale or spoils (counts fridge, freezer and the sandbox).
- New manual page: **Inventory: All nearby and search**.

## 0.7.0

- **Compass bar** now sits in the bottom right corner. Drag it with the mouse to move it; the spot is saved for every save.
- **Close and reopen the compass**: right-click the bar > **Close the compass** (or **Move the compass back to its default spot**). Reopen it by right-clicking the compass or the GPS in your inventory, from the world's **Better Experience** menu or with the hotkey.
- Without a GPS in your inventory, the sign and the bar no longer show the "needs a GPS" warning. The destination stays saved.
- **Inventory item size** (new option, from 60 to 150; 100 = the game's size): makes the rows and icons in the inventory and loot lists smaller.
- **Admin panel** (off by default): items with accent-free search, powers (ghost, god mode, free flight) and status (heal, buffs and debuffs). On a server it only opens for admin roles.

## 0.6.0

- **Auto foraging**: with the game's search mode on, the character walks to the items found and picks them up, near where it started. It walks to the "eye" so the search reveals the item. Right-clicking a found item lets you pick only that item or only that category.
- **Foraging filter**: everything, everything but junk, food only, fishing bait only, medicinal only or materials only.
- **Auto discard** (off by default): revealed items that do not pass the filter disappear from the search.
- **Speed up automatic tasks** (off by default): automatic fishing and organizing run at x5, x20 or x40 and go back to normal at the end or when a zombie comes. Single player only.
- **Take food off the heat when done**: with the character nearby and free, food that finished cooking in a stove, oven, microwave, fireplace, barbecue or campfire goes to the inventory before it burns.

## 0.5.1

- **Compass bar**: hover a favorite to see its name. While navigating, the arrow sign goes away (the destination is on the bar).
- **Favorites range on the bar** is now 600 tiles by default (about one town), so the bar does not get cluttered.
- The map button is now called **Better Experience**.
- **Safe pause**: new **Also when standing still** option (off by default), for leaving the game running away from the PC.

## 0.5.0

- **Multiplayer**: the mod works on servers. Drinking from bags uses the game's action, the GPS (battery and glove box) is handled by the server, and favorites, track and seen items are kept in a file on your PC.
- **Server options** for the GPS: require a powered GPS, battery life and cars with a GPS in the glove box.
- **Safe pause** in multiplayer: becomes a warning with a sound and turns off automatic fishing and organizing.

## 0.4.0

- **Mod features**: each part (organizer, food, map search, GPS, compass, drinking from bags, manual) can be turned off in the options. When off, it does not show up, does not run and writes nothing to your save.
- **GPS** (new item): favorites, destination with a map line and an on-screen arrow, track recording. The battery only drains while navigating or recording.
- **Compass bar** at the top of the screen, with the cardinal points and your favorites, while holding a compass or with the GPS on.
- A GPS in the glove box of a few cars (1% by default).
- Map item search in Portuguese and English.
- **Drink from bags**: the game's auto-drink also uses water in your bags.
- In-game **mod manual**, written by the community, with player tips and diagrams.

## 0.3.0

- **Map item search**: the mod records what was inside furniture you have seen and shows on the map where each item is.

## 0.2.0

- Two-level categories (group and subcategory). Food split into freezer, fridge and pantry.
- Rotten or burnt items go to the trash; raw food that was cooked goes to ready meals.

## 0.1.0

- **Base organizer**: tag furniture with a category and store your inventory or organize the whole base.
- **Food by expiration**: a list of what expires first.
- Options and hotkeys.

# Options

Every option is in **Options > Mods > Better Experience - ALL In One**. Below, each one with its factory value.

## Mod features

Turn on only what you want to use. A feature that is off leaves the menus, the map and the hotkeys, stops running and writes nothing new to your save. What was already saved (tagged furniture, recorded containers, favorites) is kept and comes back if you turn it on again.

- **Base organizer** (default: on): Tag furniture with a category, store your inventory and organize the base. Also shows in the inventory where each item would go.
- **Food by expiration** (default: on): Window with the food in your base and inventory, from what expires first to what lasts longest.
- **Map item search** (default: on): Records what is inside containers you have seen and lets you search them on the map. When off, nothing is recorded.
- **GPS** (default: on): GPS item, favorites, navigation and track. When off, the GPS leaves building and glove box loot (applies when the save loads) and the battery does not drain. A GPS already in the world stays, but does nothing.
- **Compass bar on screen** (default: on): Shows the cardinal points with an equipped compass or a powered GPS. Favorites show when the GPS feature is on; distance only with a powered GPS. Drag the bar to move it; right-click it to close.
- **Auto-drink from bags** (default: on): With the game's "Auto drink" option on, the character also drinks water carried in backpacks and bags, not only from the main inventory.
- **Mod manual** (default: on): In-game community manual, from the mod menu or its hotkey.
- **Automatic fishing** (default: on): Right-click on water: fishes on its own, eats, drinks and stores the fish in the tagged containers. See **Automatic fishing**.
- **Auto foraging** (default: on): With the game's search mode on: the character walks to the items found and picks them up, near where it started. See **Auto foraging**.
- **Take food off the heat when done** (default: on): Character nearby and free: food that finished cooking in a stove, oven, microwave, fireplace, barbecue or campfire goes to the inventory before it burns. See **Take food off the heat when done**.
- **Admin panel** (default: off): Items with accent-free search, powers (ghost, god mode, free flight) and status (heal, buffs and debuffs), plus the **Mobs** tab, which spawns zombies (normal, crawler, playing dead, sitting) and animals of every breed near you. In single player it is cheating, so it starts off. The first time you open the panel in a save, it asks first; if you confirm, the save is marked and the panel is always available in that save, even if you turn this option off. On a server it only opens for admin roles. Open it with right-click > **Better Experience** > **Admin panel** or with the hotkey.
- **Zombie health bar** (default: on): Shows a small health bar above nearby zombies you can see, and the damage of each hit rising above them. See **Zombie health bar**.
- **Highlight combat area** (default: on): While aiming a melee weapon, a circle on the ground shows the reach of the swing and the zombies it would hit get an outline in the same color. See **Highlight combat area**.
- **Combat area color** (default: red): Red or yellow, for the circle and the outline.
- **Pause when a zombie gets close** (default: on): Pauses the game if a zombie comes near while the character is doing something on its own (automatic fishing, organizing, reading, crafting, reloading, foraging). See **Pause when a zombie gets close**.
- **Zombie distance that pauses** (default: 15, from 5 to 40): In tiles. Counts the same floor and the ones right above and below.
- **Also when standing still** (default: off): Also pauses (or warns, in multiplayer) when a zombie comes near and the character is standing still (not walking and not aiming). Good for leaving the game running away from the PC. Off by default: in danger, you decide.

## Automatic tasks

Speed of the tasks the character does alone and what auto foraging picks up. Auto foraging starts from the world right-click menu (with search mode on), from the right-click on an item found by the search, or from its key.

- **Speed up automatic tasks** (default: off; x5, x20 or x40): Automatic fishing and organizing run at this speed and go back to normal when they end or when a zombie comes. While walking it stays at x5 at most. Foraging is not sped up. Single player only. See **Speed up automatic tasks**.
- **Auto foraging radius** (default: 10, from 3 to 25): Distance (tiles) around where foraging started: the character does not go past it.
- **What auto foraging picks up** (default: everything): Everything, everything but junk, food only, fishing bait only, medicinal only or materials only (firewood, stones...). Right-clicking an item found by the search lets you pick only that item or only that category. Plants the character knows are poisonous are skipped.
- **Auto discard** (default: off): While auto foraging, items the search reveals that do not pass the filter disappear from the search instead of staying on screen. The item is not picked up. In multiplayer it disappears only for you.

## Inventory

- **All nearby button in the loot window** (default: on): Adds a button to the loot window's container column that shows the items of every nearby container (and the floor) in one list. It is always the first button and outlines the boxes in the list. It appears when there are 2 or more containers nearby. See **Inventory: All nearby and search**.
- **Search bar in the inventories** (default: on): A text box in the title bar of the inventory and the loot window that filters the list. No accents needed; finds the item by its name in English too and by category.
- **Freshness bar in the food tooltip** (default: on): The end of the food tooltip shows a freshness bar and how long until it stops being fresh or spoils. It counts a powered fridge, the freezer and the sandbox rot speed.
- **Trash in the inventory** (default: on): Last icon of the inventory column, stuck to the bottom. An item dropped there stops weighing and is gone for good after 10 minutes; until then you can take it back. See **Inventory: All nearby and search**.
- **Inventory item size (percent)** (default: 100, from 60 to 150): Size of the rows and icons in the inventory and loot lists. 100 is the game's size; lower fits more items in the window. Changes right away. A row never gets smaller than its text: to shrink further, set **Inventory Font** to Small in the game options.

## Organizer

Tag containers with right-click > BetterExperience > Category. Favorite, equipped, worn, hotbar items and keys are never moved.

- **Base radius (tiles)** (default: 20, from 5 to 40): Distance from your character in which tagged containers are used.
- **Floors above/below** (default: 2, from 0 to 4): How many floors above and below your character count as base (includes basements).
- **Organize bag contents** (default: on): Also stores the items inside the backpack and bags you carry.
- **Stop if zombies are near** (default: on): Interrupts organizing when a zombie chases you or gets very close.

## Item search on the map

The assistant records what is inside containers the game has already shown you (when walking by). Then click Items on the map to search.

- **Record seen containers** (default: on): Turn off to stop recording (existing records stay).
- **Update radius** (default: 8, from 0 to 15): Every 3 s, re-record already seen containers within this radius (tiles). 0 = only those shown in the loot panel.

## GPS

Equipped compass: a bar on screen (Skyrim style, bottom right; you can drag it) with the cardinal points and your favorites. Powered GPS: creates and manages favorites, shows distance, navigates (map line and arrow) and records your track; it also turns the bar on. The GPS is found in electronics and camping stores, military storage, survival gear and in the glove box of a few cars.

In multiplayer, **Require a powered GPS**, **Battery life** and **Cars with a GPS in the glove box** come from the server options (see **Multiplayer**).

- **Require a powered GPS** (default: on): Off: favorites, navigation and track work without the item.
- **Battery life (in-game hours of use)** (default: 48, from 6 to 240): The battery only drains while navigating or recording.
- **Cars with a GPS in the glove box (%)** (default: 1, from 0 to 5): Rolled once per car, the first time you open the glove box. Not affected by the save's loot rarity.
- **Compass must be held** (default: on): On: hold the compass (the off hand works). Off: having it in your inventory, a bag or the key ring is enough.
- **Favorites range on the bar (tiles)** (default: 500, from 50 to 5000): Only favorites closer than this show on the bar (500 is about one town), so far-away ones do not clutter it. They stay on the map, and the GPS destination is always shown.

## Manual

- **Show community tips** (default: on): At the end of each manual page, shows the tips players sent. They are not part of the official guide.

## Hotkeys

- **Store inventory at base** (default: no key): Press again to stop.
- **Organize the whole base** (default: no key): Press again to stop.
- **Toggle food by expiration** (default: no key)
- **Open item search on map** (default: no key)
- **Open favorites / GPS on the map** (default: no key)
- **Show/hide the compass bar** (default: no key)
- **Open/close the manual** (default: no key)
- **Fish automatically / stop** (default: no key): Starts automatic fishing on the water under the mouse, or stops it.
- **Auto foraging / stop** (default: no key): Starts auto foraging (turns the game's search mode on if needed), or stops it.
- **Admin panel** (default: no key): Opens or closes the admin panel (needs the **Admin panel** feature on, or a save where the panel was already used).

> No key is bound by default, so the mod never clashes with other mods.

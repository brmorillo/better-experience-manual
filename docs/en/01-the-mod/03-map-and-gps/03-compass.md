# Compass

Hold the game's **compass** in your hand (the off hand works). A Skyrim-style bar shows up at the top of the screen with the cardinal points and your **favorites**.

- When you look towards a favorite, its name shows.
- **^** and **v** mean the point is on another floor.
- With a powered GPS the bar also shows, with distances.

The compass **does not create favorites**. For that you need the GPS.

## Options

- **Compass bar at the top of the screen**: turns the bar on or off.
- **Compass must be held**: when off, having the compass in your inventory, a bag or the key ring is enough.
- **Favorites range on the bar**: farther favorites do not show.

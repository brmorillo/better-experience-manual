# Compass

Hold the game's **compass** in your hand (the off hand works). A bar shows up with the cardinal points and your nearby **favorites**, in the bottom right corner of the screen.

- When you look towards a favorite, its name shows.
- Hover the mouse over a favorite on the bar to see its name.
- While navigating with the GPS, the destination is on the bar (green square). The arrow sign only shows when the bar is off or hidden.
- **^** and **v** mean the point is on another floor.
- With a powered GPS the bar also shows, with distances.

## Moving and closing it

- **Move**: hold the left mouse button on the bar and drag it. The spot is saved and works for every save.
- **Close**: right-click the bar > **Close the compass**. The same menu has **Move the compass back to its default spot** to undo the move.
- **Reopen**: right-click the compass or the GPS in your inventory > **Show the compass**. The option also shows in the **Better Experience** menu when you right-click the world, while the bar is closed.
- The hotkey (**Show/hide the compass bar**, in the options) also closes and reopens it.

The compass **does not create favorites**. For that you need the GPS.

## Options

- **Compass bar on screen**: turns the bar on or off.
- **Compass must be held**: when off, having the compass in your inventory, a bag or the key ring is enough.
- **Favorites range on the bar**: farther favorites do not show on the bar (default 500 tiles, about one town). They stay on the map.

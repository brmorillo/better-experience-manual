# Zombie health bar

A small health bar shows above nearby zombies you can see, and the damage of each hit rises above them.

## The bar

- Shows on zombies up to **12 tiles** away, on the same floor, that your character **can see**.
- **Green** at full health, **yellow** in the middle and **red** near the end.
- Each zombie spawns with a different health. A full bar is the most health that zombie ever had.

## The damage

On each hit, the damage rises above the zombie and fades in a little over 1 second:

- **yellow**: the damage of the hit;
- **red**: the killing blow.

The number is the health the zombie lost, times 100.

## Options

- **Options > Mods > Better Experience - ALL In One > Mod features > Zombie health bar**: turns the bar and the damage on and off.

# Automatic fishing

The character stays at the water's edge and fishes on its own: casts, reels the fish in, eats, drinks and stores the fish at the base. You come back whenever you want.

## How to use

1. Carry a **fishing rod with a hook** and **bait** in your inventory.
2. **Right-click the water** > **Better Experience - ALL In One** > **Fish here automatically**.

You can also use the **Fish automatically / stop** hotkey: it starts fishing on the water under the mouse. The key is not set by default; pick one in **Options > Mods > Better Experience - ALL In One > Hotkeys**.

If something is missing, the menu option is greyed out and its tooltip tells you why.

## How to stop

- **Walk** with the character.
- Or right-click > **Better Experience - ALL In One** > **Stop automatic fishing**.
- Or press the hotkey again.

When it stops, it tells you how many fish you caught.

## What you need

- A **fishing rod** in your inventory, with a **hook**.
- **Bait**: the one already on the rod or one from your inventory. The mod uses an **artificial lure** first (it is rarely used up), then **worms and insects**, and last any food that works as bait. **Favorite** or equipped items are never used as bait.
- Stand **on the ground** (ground floor), within **15 tiles** of the water and outside vehicles.

## What it does while fishing

- **Picks the spot**: aims at the tile in range with the **most fish** (the fish schools, where the water bubbles). When it can, it prefers tiles **more than 7 tiles from the shore**, where medium and large fish are not rarer. See **Fishing** under Skills.
- **Minds the line**: reels without letting the tension get close to the point where the line breaks. If nothing bites for a while, it casts again.
- **Trash**: any trash it catches is dropped on the ground.
- **Drinks**: when thirsty, it drinks from any water you carry, including bottles inside your backpack.
- **Eats**: when hungry, it eats **ready food**, always the one that spoils first. It does not eat if already full.
- **Stores the fish**: with **3 fish**, or with your inventory above **75%** of its weight, it takes the fish to the nearest tagged container that accepts fish and returns to the same spot. For that, tag a container with the **Base organizer** (for example, a freezer as **Freezer**). The fish goes to the same place the organizer would send it.

### What counts as ready food

- It needs no cooking and no opening.
- It is not rotten, burnt, frozen or poisoned.
- It is not alcohol, a spice, bait or a favorite item.

## When it stops on its own

- A zombie is chasing you or gets very close.
- You get into a vehicle.
- The bait runs out, or the rod is gone (the line broke).
- Hunger or thirst gets strong and there is nothing ready to eat or no water to drink.
- Your inventory is almost full (95%) and there is nowhere to store the fish.
- It cannot get back to the fishing spot.

In every case a message tells you why.

With **Pause when a zombie gets close** on, the game also pauses when a zombie approaches. See **Pause when a zombie gets close**.

## Options

- **Options > Mods > Better Experience - ALL In One > Mod features > Automatic fishing**: turns it on and off.
- **Hotkeys > Fish automatically / stop**: no key by default.

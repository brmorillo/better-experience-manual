# Journal, XP box and Survival Guide

## Journal

The **Journal** keeps a summary of every day of the character, by the game calendar. Open it in the right-click menu: **BetterExperience > Journal**.

- On the left, the days (newest on top). On the right, the summary of the chosen day.
- **Combat**: zombies killed.
- **Skills**: the XP gained in each skill and the levels gained (**level 2 to 3**).
- **Day to day**: distance walked and driven, hours slept, meals, drinks and recipes made.
- **Health**: new bites, deep wounds, cuts and scratches, and the lowest health of the day.
- **Today** is counted live. When the day turns, a message says that yesterday's summary is ready.
- It is saved with the character, in the save. Each character has its own journal. It keeps the last 400 days.
- It only starts counting after the mod is installed: older days do not appear.

## Floating XP box

When the character gains XP, a small box appears on the screen with each skill going up:

- The skill, its **level** and how much XP came in (**+12.5 XP**), adding up while you keep gaining.
- The **bar of the current level** and **how much is left** for the next one (**42 XP left for level 3**).
- The most recent skill on top, up to 5 at once.
- It disappears by itself a few seconds after the XP stops. Drag it with the mouse to place it anywhere.

## Skip the Survival Guide

The game opens the **Survival Guide** and pauses every time a save loads. With the **Skip the Survival Guide on load** option it no longer opens by itself. **F1** still opens it whenever you want, and the game's options do not change.

## Options

- **Options > Mods > Better Experience - ALL In One > Floating XP box**: turns it on and off (default: on).

- **Options > Mods > Better Experience - ALL In One > Journal (summary of each day)**: turns it on and off (default: on).
- **Options > Mods > Better Experience - ALL In One > Skip the Survival Guide on load**: turns it on and off (default: on).

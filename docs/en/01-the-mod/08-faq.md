# FAQ

## I only want one part of the mod

Turn the rest off in **Options > Mods > Better Experience - ALL In One > Mod features**. Whatever is off does not show up, does not run and writes nothing new to your save. You can turn it back on any time: what was already saved is still there.

## My character does not store anything

Check that your furniture is tagged with a category and inside the **base radius** (option). Favorite, equipped and hotbar items are never moved.

## Map search cannot find an item I know is there

The search only knows containers you have **seen up close**. Walk by the furniture and open the loot panel once.

## The compass bar does not show

Check that the **Compass bar on screen** feature is on. Hold the compass in your hand, or turn off **Compass must be held** in the options. If you closed the bar (right-click on it or the hotkey), reopen it by right-clicking the compass in your inventory > **Show the compass**. It sits in the bottom right corner unless you moved it.

## I cannot create favorites

Favorites need the **GPS** feature on and a **powered GPS** in your inventory. The compass only shows favorites that already exist, and only with the GPS feature on.

## Does it work in multiplayer?

Yes. Install it on the server and on every player. A few features change a little: the safe pause becomes a warning, and your favorites are kept on your PC. See **Multiplayer**.

## Can I add the mod to an existing save?

Yes. Categories and favorites are stored in your save.

## I turned the GPS off but still found one

Turning it off removes the GPS from building and glove box loot from the next time you load the save. Ones that were already generated stay in the world, but do nothing while the feature is off.

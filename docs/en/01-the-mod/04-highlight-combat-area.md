# Highlight combat area

While you aim a melee weapon, a circle on the ground around your character shows the reach of the swing. The zombies the swing would hit right now get a light outline in the same color.

## How to use

1. Equip a melee weapon.
2. Hold the **right mouse button** to aim.

The circle and the outlines go away when you let go of the button. Nothing shows with a firearm or inside a car.

The color (**red** or **yellow**) is in **Options > Mods > Better Experience - ALL In One > Mod features > Combat area color**.

## How the game decides who gets hit

These are the game's own rules (Build 42). The mod only shows what they give.

- **Reach**: the weapon's maximum range. A baseball bat, for example, reaches 1.25 tiles.
- **Skill level 7**: with **Long Blunt**, **Axe** or **Spear** at level 7 or higher, the weapons of that skill reach **20% farther**. The circle grows with it.
- **To the closest body part**: the game measures from your character to the zombie's head, hips or feet, whichever is closest (when it is lying down, the torso too). So the real reach **changes a little with the zombie's size and posture**: a tall zombie, or one bent toward you with its head forward, gets hit a bit before its feet enter the circle. A zombie lying down can be hit by its head or torso.
- **Lunge**: a zombie lunging at you within 2 tiles counts as 0.2 tiles closer.
- **Fan in front**: the swing only hits what is inside a fan in front of your character. Each weapon has its own width: a baseball bat hits up to about 41° to each side. For a zombie **lying down** the fan is wider (about 60° to each side with the bat).
- **Same floor**: only zombies on your floor get hit. Distance only counts along the ground; height does not.

## The outline is an approximation

The mod measures to the zombie's feet, not to the closest body part. An outline means the distance is already enough for the swing to hit. At the edge of the circle, a tall or bent zombie can get hit before it is outlined, and right at the edge of the fan the result can vary a little.

## What about the game's own outline?

The game has the option **Melee Target Outline** (Options > User Interface). It outlines in red exactly who the swing hits, measuring to the body parts. When it is on, the mod only draws the circle and leaves the outline to the game.

## Options

- **Options > Mods > Better Experience - ALL In One > Mod features > Highlight combat area**: turns it on and off.
- **Combat area color**: red or yellow.

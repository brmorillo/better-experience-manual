# Tag your furniture

Right-click a piece of furniture (fridge, cabinet, crate, shelf...) > **BetterExperience** > **Categories: ...**. When you hover the option, the furniture gets outlined in the world; with several pieces on the same spot, repeated names get a number (**Crate 1**, **Crate 2**).

The option opens the categories window. The furniture stays outlined while it is open. Check one or more:

- **A whole group**, like **All Fridge** or **All Tools**: takes anything from that group. Click **+** or **-** to open the group and see its subcategories.
- **A subcategory**, like **Canned and preserved** or **Ammo**: takes only that.
- **Inbox**: a place to drop things without thinking. The assistant empties it later. It does not mix with other categories.
- **Trash / spoiled**: rotten food and junk go there.
- **General**: anything without a better place.

On the right of each row you see how many items of that category are already in the furniture. The buttons at the top help:

- **Show only categories already in the box**: hides the rest of the list.
- **Check the ones in the box**: checks exactly the categories of what is already stored there. Good for a box you filled by hand.
- **Uncheck all**.

Click **Save** to keep it. With nothing checked, the furniture has no category.

Right-click an item in your inventory: the menu shows **Better Experience stores in: ...**.
